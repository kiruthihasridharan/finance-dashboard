Overview of the Project
The Finance Dashboard (finance-dashboard) is a personal investment portfolio tracking and wealth management web application built with Next.js and Material UI (MUI).

The application consolidates holdings, tracks real-time portfolio metrics, visualizes asset allocation, logs transactions, and tracks progress towards net worth goals.

Key Features & Components
Portfolio KPI Metrics: Calculates total portfolio value, total gain/loss ($ and %), estimated dividend income, and progress toward financial goals in real-time.
Historical Performance Chart: An interactive performance graph supporting 1M, YTD, and 5Y timeframe views.
Holdings Table: Displays detailed investment rows (Tickers, Accounts, Share counts, Prices, Gain/Loss) with price adjustments.
Asset & Sector Allocation: Visual breakdown across asset classes (Equity, ETF, Crypto), sectors, and accounts using linear progress bars.
Movers & Goal Tracking: Highlights top gainers and losers, and tracks progress against net worth milestones.
Transaction Manager & Activity Feed: Allows users to log Buy, Sell, and Dividend transactions, automatically updating portfolio balances and cost bases.
